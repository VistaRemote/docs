      #WebRTC topology

      VistaRemote defaults to P2P + STUN/TURN (coturn) for 1:1 remote desktop. Use mediasoup SFU when multiple controllers watch one agent, P2P keeps failing, or TURN relay load is too high.

      Commercial: SFU is included during the 7-day standard_7d trial; after trial you need Pro or higher. See Trial and paid features.

      Normative spec: Meta-Repo spec/webrtc-architecture-spec.md

      #Why not SRS?

      FactorRationale
      Use caseRemote desktop & enterprise monitoring, not mass live streaming
      ScaleTens of concurrent sessions — mediasoup sidecar is enough
      StackTypeScript end-to-end, same ops model as server/ai
      CostSRS adds a separate C++ media plane we do not need

      #Mode matrix

      ScenarioModeMedia path
      1 controller : 1 agentP2PEnd-to-end; coturn relay if needed
      1 agent : N controllersSFUSingle uplink to mediasoup; N downlinks
      Repeated ICE failureSFUServer policy fallback
      coturn overloadSFUNew sessions prefer SFU to spare TURN
