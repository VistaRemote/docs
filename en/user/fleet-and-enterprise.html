      #1. What the product includes

      VistaRemote is more than one-to-one remote desktop. For enterprises and edge sites, it includes a full Device Fleet capability set:

      CapabilityIn one lineTypical use
      1:1 remote desktopHigh-quality view + keyboard/mouse + files / terminalSupport desks, break/fix
      Group keyboard & mouseOne controller operates many authorized devices in syncLab tours, mass config, training
      Batch commandsNotify, lock, reboot, open URL across devicesEnd-of-day lock, alerts, incident response
      Multi-screen monitor wallLow-cost thumbnail observe; click into HD controlNOC walls, edge-site patrol
      Virtual displayDedicated virtual screen for remote sessionsHelpdesk remote, unattended stations
      Out-of-band (OOB)Intel AMT power / BIOS / KVM (enterprise private deploy)Machines that are powered off or pre-OS
      Audit & RBACTraceable actions, org rolesCompliance, IT separation of duties

      With VistaCast: Cast watches business video; Remote manages edge compute boxes — a natural B2B bundle (cameras + Edge AI Box + VistaRemote management).

      #2. Who gets it and how to enable

      Capability familyPlanHow to enable
      Core 1:1 remoteFree / Pro / EnterpriseDefault product path
      Fleet: group control, batch commands, wall, virtual displayEnterprisePurchase enterprise service, then enable entitlements
      OOB / AMTEnterprise + private deploymentDefined in the contract and delivery scope

      Sales & delivery posture (product commitment)

      • The capabilities above are in product scope: you can present them in proposals and RFPs as “supported.”
      • After an enterprise purchase or signed contract, the team enables, tunes, or add-ons to the site’s needs. Some capabilities are delivered and accepted on the contract’s schedule.
      • Endpoint management can be priced on its own (per device / seat), not only as a remote-desktop checkbox.

      Pricing and enablement: contact sales or your implementer. Overview: Plans & billing.

      #3. Monitor wall: observe cheaply, control when needed

      Thumbnail wall (low-cost observe)
        ↓ anomaly
Click device (enterprise entitlement)
        ↓
High-quality Remote Session (control)
      ModePurpose
      Thumbnail observeLow resolution / frame rate preview across many online devices
      High-quality controlClick into a 720p / 1080p-class remote session

      This separates “100 devices online” from “100 simultaneous HD streams.” The latter is a contract add-on, not a default catalog promise. Larger walls (market messaging often cites tens to ~100+ observe slots) can be enabled and validated under contract on the existing architecture.

      Entry: Admin Monitor wall; click a live slot to open a control session. See Admin console.

      #4. Group control & batch commands

      #Batch commands

      From My devices (Client) or Admin Devices, multi-select devices and send:

      • Desktop notification
      • Lock screen
      • Reboot / shutdown (typed confirm required)
      • Open an http(s) URL

      Each device returns its own result; actions are audited.

      #Group keyboard & mouse

      On My devices, select online computers and open multi-computer control (/control), then switch to Sync. Keyboard and mouse on the focused picture go to every device that has tapped Allow. Clipboard, files, and the terminal are not broadcast. Agents can disconnect at any time; unauthorized devices are never controlled implicitly. Without the group-control entitlement, only Focus mode is available.

      #5. Virtual display

      The product supports a virtual display for remote sessions so work happens on a dedicated virtual screen and disturbs the physical desktop less. It works with privacy-screen and multi-display policies.

      • Enable after the enterprise virtual display entitlement is granted (Agent UI).
      • Windows is the priority delivery platform; other OSes follow the contract.
      • Driver / imaging integrations can be delivered after purchase against the site design.

      #6. Out-of-band (AMT and similar)

      For powered-off or pre-OS machines, Enterprise private deployments can include out-of-band management (e.g. Intel AMT: power, BIOS, OOB KVM). The default cloud path focuses on online Agents; OOB is enabled under enterprise contract + private deployment.
