## Which installer?

| Your situation | Package |
| :--- | :--- |
| Personal remote help | **Consumer** (public download) |
| Company policy on your own laptop | **Enterprise BYOD** (IT portal/link) |
| Company-issued PC | **Enterprise Managed** (IT preinstall; you may not download) |

## After install

### Consumer

- You start sharing explicitly; then you get a code, QR, or link.
- Each session usually asks for confirmation again.

### Enterprise BYOD

- Sign in with your **org account** and accept the **consent** screen.
- If policy allows, the agent may **auto-enroll** for IT monitoring without clicking share every time.
- You can uninstall; the org may be notified.

### Enterprise Managed

- Agent runs per company policy, often **auto-monitoring** after login.
- Pairing UI may be **hidden** from employees (IT-assisted sessions only).
- Uninstall typically requires admin rights.

## How controllers connect

They use your **numeric code**, **QR scan**, or **session link** — all equivalent. See [Remote control](/user/remote-control).

## Compliance

Enterprise installers show a **disclosure** on first run. Follow your organization’s contract and privacy policy.
