# Open source and commercial licensing

## Two tracks

| Use | Fee | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal, non-commercial** | **Free** | Learning and hobby use under the root [LICENSE](https://github.com/VistaRemote/VistaRemote/blob/main/LICENSE) |
| **Any commercial use** | **Paid license required** | Internal business use, SaaS, embedding in products, paid hosting, etc. |

Commercial use is defined by **for-profit activity**, not only by company registration.

## Versus Pro / Enterprise plans

- **LICENSE**: whether you may **deploy, modify, and redistribute** the code.
- **Plans**: **feature gates** on your deployment or our SaaS (recording, SFU, cloud AI, etc.).

Self-hosted commercial deployments typically need **both** a commercial license and the right subscription tier.

## Model fine-tuning (closed source)

Training pipelines, customer datasets, and proprietary LoRA adapters belong in a **separate private repo** (`ai-finetune`), delivered as encrypted **model bundles**. The public `ai` repo handles inference and orchestration only.

See Meta-Repo `spec/licensing-spec.md` and `spec/ai-finetune-spec.md`.
